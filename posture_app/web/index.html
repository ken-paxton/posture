    Calibrate your baseline

    1. Sit all the way back so the chair supports your lower back; feet flat on the floor.
    2. Let your shoulders relax down. Don't pull them back or puff your chest out.
    3. Head balanced over your shoulders, chin level, eyes on the middle of the screen.
    4. Screen at about arm's length, with its top line at or slightly below eye level.
    5. Breathe normally. It should feel easy to hold, not effortful.
